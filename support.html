❓ Common questions

I bought GhostBoard Pro before. What happens now?

Nothing changes for you: all features stay unlocked and your saved themes are kept. You no longer need your license key. If you have a question about a past purchase, email ghostboard.support@gmail.com.

How do I change the board or pieces?

Click the GhostBoard icon in Chrome's toolbar → Settings, then pick a board, piece set, or preset and click Save. An open Chess.com tab updates automatically.

The GhostBoard overlay isn't showing up

Make sure you're on a supported practice page: Play vs Computer, Play vs Coach, Puzzles, Analysis, Lessons, Courses, Endgames, or Practice. GhostBoard intentionally stays off in live games, daily games, tournaments, and all rated play against people, to respect Chess.com Fair Play. If you're on a supported page, try refreshing the tab.

The board is out of sync

Click Reset on the overlay to snap back to the current Chess.com position, or refresh the page.
